"Most estates are designed for how they photograph. Aurelia Grove was designed for how it lives — hour to hour, light to light."

Aurelia Grove is a collection of eight three-storey pool villas set within a private soi off the Bang Na–Trat corridor, roughly eighteen minutes from Thonglor by expressway. Each residence pairs a climate-controlled two-car showroom garage with an adjoining lounge, so the collection and the living space share one continuous floor.

Above, the plan opens into a double-height living hall, a courtyard pool, and a top-floor suite with its own roof garden — a rhythm of rooms built to be lived in, not just viewed.
